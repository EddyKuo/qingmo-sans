FONTLOG for Qingmo Sans TC (晴墨黑體)
=====================================

This file provides detailed information on the Qingmo Sans TC font software.
This information should be distributed along with the font software and any
derivative works.


Basic Font Information
----------------------

Qingmo Sans TC is a Traditional Chinese text typeface that blends a sans-serif
(hei) skeleton with the stroke rhythm of Ming (song) type. It is a derivative of
Source Han Sans TC and is released under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.

Base font: Source Han Sans TC (Variable), Copyright 2014-2021 Adobe
(http://www.adobe.com/), with Reserved Font Name 'Source'. In accordance with
the OFL, no Qingmo font name contains the Reserved Font Name.


ChangeLog
---------

2026-10-30 (Qingmo Sans Project) Version 0.103
- Kaishu brush feel made visible and extended to horizontals and verticals,
  after the maintainer reported that the dots of 火, 煷, 犕, 痻 and the
  horizontal and vertical strokes still lacked kaishu brush rhythm. A new
  structural transform, brush, runs after strokes and before corners:
  - heng (horizontal, 勒): entry end's top corner raised 12% of the end
    width with a slight round (15%); exit end's top corner raised 22% and
    bottom corner lowered 12% (the press down-right), rounded 35%; free
    standing horizontals get a thinner waist (12%). 38,410 entry and
    42,174 exit ends; 31,943 waists
  - shu (vertical, 努): entry end's right corner pushed out 10% with a
    round (30%); exit end is chuilu (垂露, dewdrop: widened to 106%,
    rounded 45%) or xuanzhen (懸針, needle: tapers over 3x the width to
    18%) for a free-standing vertical at least 0.45 UPM long, centred
    (30-70% of the advance), reaching the bottom of the glyph, crossing
    another stroke with at least 2.5x its width showing above, and with
    no curved stroke touching it: 中十年平華車申半 are needles, 下不本木東
    are dewdrops. 30,116 entry ends, 23,162 chuilu, 560 xuanzhen
  - Main and minor strokes: each end's strength ramps from brush_minor
    (stroke length below 45% of the longest stroke of the same direction
    in the glyph) to full (90% and above), so only the main strokes carry
    the full press and 三's short upper strokes stay light
  - All amounts are fractions of the end width in each master
- Two style profiles, built side by side at the maintainer's request after
  asking why the result still did not look beautiful (config/spec.yaml
  "profiles", selected with make PROFILE=<name>):
  - subtle (default, direction 1: hei with a little, consistent brush
    feel): brush at 60%, minor strokes at 35%, dot press 112%
  - kaishu (direction 2: kaishu-flavoured hei, changes the skeleton):
    brush at 110%, minor strokes at 45%, free-standing horizontals sheared
    up 2 deg to the right (左低右高), horizontal / vertical 0.82 -> 0.76,
    waist 16%, dot press 125% and round 70%
- Dots: the bottom end is now widened to 125% (dot_press_pct) before the
  round is added, so the press of the brush is visible. Dots that were
  missed: near-vertical dots up to 0.42 UPM now qualify unless they lean
  less than 2.5 deg (the slanted vertical of 弓 is 2 deg; the old limit
  was 12 deg, which excluded the 火 radical and the longer 灬 dots at about
  7 deg); near-vertical left dots (忄) may be 0.30 UPM long when they lean
  at least 8 deg. 41,967 dots in total (+1,221)
- zhuo (short pie, 啄): a straight 4-segment stroke falling to the left,
  0.25-0.36 UPM long, that is not a ti, e.g. the right stroke of 火, gets
  the ti template reversed (round, widened head at the top right, narrow
  tip at the bottom left); 280 strokes
- Safety net: subtle 116 of 17,684 glyphs skip a transform (was 118; brush
  never skipped); kaishu 119 (brush skipped in 𤨕 鰳)
- VF shrinks by about 1.95 MB (subtle 17,924,848 bytes, kaishu 17,917,228):
  rounded ends are no longer softened by corners, which added two points
  per corner; brush adds one point per end
- Gray std ratio 0.921 -> subtle 0.929 / kaishu 0.934 (limit 1.05); ink
  coverage 0.295 -> 0.302 / 0.303

2026-10-29 (Qingmo Sans Project) Version 0.102
- Kaishu (small regular script) brush feel, subtle strength, approved by the
  maintainer after reviewing 0.101 (see Design Spec, "楷書筆意"). Based only on
  the textual description of the Eight Principles of Yong (永字八法):
  - ti (rising stroke, 策): entry end widened to 115% with a rounded press,
    exit narrowed to 22%. Recognised as a straight 4-segment stroke rising
    12-75 deg, 0.25-0.48 UPM long, in the left part of the glyph (left
    radicals such as 氵冫扌土王牛足金); strength ramps from 0 at 5 deg to
    full at 25 deg, so flat ti (金 11-14 deg, 虫, 王) get only a little;
    5,256 strokes, 1,584 of them at full strength
  - changdian (long dot, 側): head narrowed to 35%, tail widened to 115%
    with a rounded end. Recognised as a 4-segment stroke falling to the
    right (-70 to -20 deg), longer than a dot and up to 0.85 UPM, straight
    head, and a straight tail or straight sides (a na has a curved tail and
    curved sides); 346 strokes in 344 glyphs, e.g. 凶, the shortened na in
    囚季逢, and the 撇點 of 女
  - hooks (趯), every kind (vertical, bent, turning, lying, slanted,
    vertical-bent, vertical-rising): the tip narrows to 35% towards its
    inner (concave) end, the last control point of the outer edge follows
    it by 50% so the hook flicks upward, and the outer edge of the bend
    before the hook bulges outward by 12% of the tip width (the press).
    Recognised as a curved stroke end where the contour turns back by more
    than 120 deg, on a contour of at least 6 segments (the hook is joined to
    the stroke before it), pointing left (|angle| >= 160 deg) or up-right
    (20-100 deg); 5,685 hooks in 5,260 glyphs. Only existing points move.
    The 宀 / 冖 hooks point down-left and already use the pie taper
- Dots: near-vertical dots now get the full dot template (pointed head,
  round end) once they lean more than 0.2 deg off vertical and are at most
  0.26 UPM long; base vertical strokes are exactly 0 deg. Near-horizontal
  dots ramp from 8 to 25 deg. Reported by the maintainer: the dots under
  糸 and 灬 (also 馬魚鳥), and the inner dots of the 金 radical, lean only
  0.4-14 deg and kept square ends under the old 12 deg threshold, so the
  same component differed between 錄 and 鏟; 2,541 more dots in 2,111
  glyphs, 40,746 in total
- Safety net unchanged: 118 of 17,684 glyphs skip a transform; no new skips
- VF grows by 67 KB (round heads add one point per dot / ti / changdian
  stroke; hooks only move existing points)
- Gray uniformity std ratio 0.918 -> 0.921 at wght 400 (limit 1.05)
- New sample charset config/charsets/strokes-sample.txt for stroke template
  regression proofs (make build CHARSET=strokes)
- Tools: scripts/qa/font_map.py (make fontmap), a single-file 2D map of every
  character with the base overlay and per-glyph review marks; make release now
  keeps build/proof/

2026-10-29 (Qingmo Sans Project) Version 0.101
- Skeleton tightened after maintainer review found 0.100 "too loose":
  counter_open_pct 3.0 -> -25.0 (centre components move inward by up to
  ~31 units, stroke widths kept; the old +3 moved them by under 4 units,
  which was not visible), center_shift_up_pct 0.5 -> 2.0 (20 units).
  Rationale: public writing on Han type design describes a tight counter and
  slightly raised centre as the traditional, more legible structure; the
  maintainer approved moving these two dimensions (see Design Spec,
  "差異化定義"). Square skeleton, soft corners and terminal rules unchanged
- Gray uniformity improved: std ratio 0.973 -> 0.918 at wght 400
- Safety net now skips 118 of 17,684 glyphs (was 109); proportion is
  skipped on 27 glyphs (was 8), none in the high-frequency 500
- Build: Makefile uses tab recipes so the GNU Make 3.81 bundled with macOS
  works; new `make release` target collects fonts into release-font/
- Distribution: web font slices (`make webfonts`: 45 unicode-range WOFF2
  slices per font ordered high-frequency 500 -> Big5 common -> Big5 less
  common -> rest, with @font-face CSS and a demo page), a Linux fontconfig
  file preferring Qingmo for zh-TW/HK/MO sans-serif and system-ui, and a
  tag-triggered GitHub Release workflow

2026-10-28 (Qingmo Sans Project) Version 0.100
- Full character set: all 17,760 Han characters of the base (Big5 13,064 plus
  HK, Ext-A and SIP characters); 18,343 glyphs
- Safety net in the transform pipeline: a transform that errors or adds a
  self-intersection at any master or interpolated weight is skipped for that
  glyph only (109 of 17,684 glyphs, listed in transform-report.json)
